    In real systems:

      How does Pip work? (and how is this different from a real AI?)

      Pip is a few dozen rules, not a language model. It looks for instruction-like phrases (a sending word, an address, a reference to the key or notes) in the text it reads, then runs the resulting action through whichever defenses are active. Real models are fuzzier and harder to predict: they follow instructions you didn't expect and ignore ones you did. That unpredictability is exactly why prompt injection can't be solved by filtering alone.

      Everything is a puzzle about a documented class of attack on a fictional agent. Nothing here contacts any real system, and the "vault key" is a made-up string.
